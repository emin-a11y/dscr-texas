What is a DSCR loan?

One ratio decides the loan.

Rent ÷ Debt

Debt service coverage ratio compares a property's rent to its monthly housing payment (PITIA). At or above 1.0, the property covers its own payment.

The asset qualifies

Underwriting looks at the property, your credit, and your down payment. Not your employer, tax returns, or how many other mortgages you hold.

Built for investors

These are business-purpose loans for investment property only, never for a primary residence.

Why it matters in Texas

Texas rentals have their own math.

Property taxes drive the ratio

With no state income tax, Texas relies heavily on property tax, and rates commonly run well above the national average. Because taxes count in the payment, they can pull a DSCR below 1.0 even on strong rent. We model it up front, no surprises at underwriting.

Insurance is part of the story

Wind, hail and flood exposure vary a lot by metro and by parcel. We help you get a realistic insurance number into your DSCR early, so the quote holds at closing.

MUDs, PIDs & HOAs

Newer Texas subdivisions often carry special-district assessments on top of tax. We account for them in the payment so your ratio is honest.

Statewide coverage

Single-family, condos, townhomes, and 2–4 unit properties across the state's major markets and many smaller ones.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you check my income?

Not personal income. Qualification rests on the property's rent, your credit, and your assets for down payment and reserves.

What if my DSCR is below 1.0?

Some programs allow it with lower leverage, a larger down payment, or a higher rate. Use the calculator above, then ask for a quote.

Can I use a short-term rental (Airbnb) income?

Often yes, using a different income method such as a market-rent analysis tool. Terms depend on the program.

Can I close in an LLC?

Yes. Many investors do. Consult an attorney about the right entity setup.

Can I use this for my home?

No. DSCR loans are for business-purpose investment property only.
